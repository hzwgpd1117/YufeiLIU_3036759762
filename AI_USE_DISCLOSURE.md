# AI-use disclosure

AI assistance was used to read and summarize the research papers and course data dictionaries, discuss the economic interpretation, design and implement the portfolio calculations, inspect intermediate outputs, draft the English reports, and organize this repository. The student selected the project and extensions, supplied the course data, requested the analyses, and remains responsible for the final submission and interpretation.

AI-generated code and text were checked against date coverage, portfolio counts, original-paper methods, and the public Kenneth French Mom factor. During checks, duplicate source observations were removed before aggregation, and a prior-calendar-year lookup error in the Table III beta grouping was corrected. The complete validation record is in [reports/06_validation_and_ai.md](reports/06_validation_and_ai.md).

The original paper's 1965–1989 results are distinguished from this project's 2002–2025 estimates. The latter are not an exact historical replication. Restricted course CRSP files are kept out of the public repository. The student should review all reported estimates, citations, and wording before submission.
